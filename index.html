CottagePaws Keyword Tool

CottagePaws Keyword Tool is an internal research tool used by the team behind CottagePaws, an online store for pet lovers and pet memorial keepsakes.

What the app does

The app connects to our own Google Ads account through the Google Ads API. It reads keyword ideas, search volume and bid estimates from Keyword Planner, and read-only performance reports for our own campaigns. We use this data to decide which products to design and how to plan our advertising.

Who uses it

Only CottagePaws staff use this app, with their own Google account. It is not offered to the public and it does not manage Google Ads accounts for other businesses.

Google account access

When a staff member signs in with Google, the app requests the Google Ads scope (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/adwords). The app only reads data. It does not create, change or delete campaigns, and it does not access Gmail, Drive, contacts or any other Google data.
