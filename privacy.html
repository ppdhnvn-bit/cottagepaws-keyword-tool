Privacy Policy

CottagePaws Keyword Tool · Last updated: November 25, 2026

This policy explains how the CottagePaws Keyword Tool ("the app") accesses, uses, stores and shares Google user data. The app is an internal tool used only by CottagePaws staff. See the app home page for what the app does.

Data the app accesses

After a staff member signs in with Google and grants the Google Ads scope (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/adwords), the app reads from our own Google Ads account:

  • Keyword ideas, average monthly searches, competition and bid estimates from Keyword Planner.
  • Read-only campaign and account reports.
  • The list of Google Ads accounts the signed-in user can access.

The app does not access Gmail, Drive, Calendar, contacts, or any Google data outside Google Ads.

How we use the data

We use the data only for our own keyword research, product planning and advertising planning. We do not use it for advertising to individuals, for profiling, or to train AI or machine-learning models.

Storage and security

The OAuth refresh token and the research results are stored on computers controlled by CottagePaws staff. They are not uploaded to third-party servers by the app. Access is limited to staff who need it.

Sharing

We do not sell Google user data and we do not share it with third parties, except where required by law.

Retention and deletion

Keyword research files are kept while they are useful for our planning and are deleted when no longer needed. A staff member can revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions; we then delete the stored token. To ask us to delete data, email support@cottagepaws.com.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
